1. POSIX Threads & Shared Mutexes

Each philosopher runs as an independent concurrent POSIX thread (pthread_create). The five forks are protected as mutual exclusion locks (pthread_mutex_t). A fork cannot be held by more than one philosopher simultaneously, preventing race conditions.

2. The Four Coffman Conditions

Deadlock can occur if and only if all four conditions hold concurrently: Mutual Exclusion, Hold & Wait, No Preemption, and Circular Wait. Eliminating any single condition guarantees deadlock prevention.

3. Resource Ordering Proof

By enforcing that every philosopher must request the lower-numbered fork before the higher-numbered fork, any Resource Allocation Graph is guaranteed to be a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG). Since a cycle cannot form in a DAG, Circular Wait is impossible.

4. Starvation Prevention & Fairness

Deadlock prevention does not guarantee starvation-freedom. In our Fair FIFO Monitor, hungry philosophers are queued in order of arrival. Even if non-conflicting philosophers dine, a philosopher waiting at the head of the queue is granted priority as soon as forks are released.
